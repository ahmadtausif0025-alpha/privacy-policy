## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Photos you upload
When you run a search, you choose a photo from your device or camera. The photo is sent securely (encrypted in transit) to our servers so the search can be performed.

- The photo is used **only** to perform the search you requested.
- To compare faces, our system may temporarily generate a mathematical representation of the face in your photo (sometimes called facial geometry or a face template). This is created **only for the duration of the search**.
- The photo and any face representation are **automatically deleted immediately after the search is completed** (and in no case kept longer than [X minutes/hours]).
- We **do not** use your photos to train or improve any facial recognition or AI model.
- We **do not** sell, rent, or share your photos or face data with anyone for their own use.

### 2.2 Search results
Search results consist of links and thumbnails of images that are already publicly available on the internet and indexed from third-party websites. We do not store the results of your searches, and we do not keep a history of what you searched for.

### 2.3 Search history
We do not keep a record of your searches on our servers. Any recent results you see inside the App exist only on your device during your session and are cleared when [you close the App / you clear them].

### 2.4 Purchases
If the App offers subscriptions or in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive limited information from Apple (such as whether a subscription is active) to provide paid features.
