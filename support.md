## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Reverse Image Search work?
Upload or take a photo, and the app searches publicly available images on the internet to find visually similar faces. Results link to the websites where those images appear.

### Do you store my photos or searches?
No. Your photo is used only to run the search and is deleted right after. We don't keep your searches or results. See our [Privacy Policy](./) for details.

### Why am I getting no results or wrong results?
- Use a clear, well-lit photo with the face looking toward the camera.
- Avoid group photos, sunglasses, heavy filters, or very small or blurry faces.
- The app can only find images that are publicly available and indexed online, so some people will have few or no matches.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**, choose [App Name], and tap **Cancel Subscription**. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the app, go to [Settings / the paywall screen], and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

### How do I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I found a photo of myself in the results. How do I remove it?
The image is hosted on a third-party website, so the best way to remove it permanently is to contact that website. To have a specific link removed from our results, email **[contact email]** with the link and we'll review your request.

### Can I use the app to identify strangers?
The app is meant for lawful uses such as finding where your own photos appear online, spotting impersonation or catfishing, and verifying image sources. Using it to stalk, harass, or track people is not allowed.
